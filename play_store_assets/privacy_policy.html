Privacy Policy for CLICKI

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the CLICKI mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the Application" or "the Service").

1. Information Collection and Storage

CLICKI respects your privacy and is designed to operate with minimal data collection:

  • Local Processing: All video downloads, media parsing, and file archiving occur on your device. Downloaded media files are stored strictly on your local device storage or device photo library.
  • No Account Required: CLICKI does not require user accounts, emails, or personal identification to use the app.
  • Clipboard Data: The application may access the clipboard only when active to detect copied media URLs. Clipboard contents are never sent to external servers.

2. Permissions

To provide full functionality, CLICKI may request the following device permissions:

  • Storage / Media Library: Required to save downloaded videos and photos directly to your device storage and Gallery.
  • Network / Internet: Required to access video URLs and download media streams requested by the user.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Application may use third-party services for advertisements and crash analytics:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, cookies, and anonymous usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

For more information on how Google uses data, visit: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your support email.
